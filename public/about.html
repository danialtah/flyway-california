    About FlyWay California

    FlyWay California helps you find primarily rare and migratory birds in California, built for birders chasing lifers and anyone with an affinity for hard-to-find birds. This project was inspired by shiny hunting in Pokémon, the thrill of finding that 1/8,192 Pokémon with a different color to all the rest is the same feeling I got when I first got into birding and found a Common Loon, my first ever rare-marked bird on eBird.

    What You Can Find

    Rarest birds
    This week's notable birds, ranked from rarest to least rare, for all of California, one county, or near you.
    Rare bird hotspots
    The eBird hotspots with the most rare birds reported recently.
    Notable sightings
    Birds eBird flags as unusual for the place or time of year, reported in the last 7 days and grouped by rarity. Only confirmed reports are shown, and the list updates every 15 minutes.
    County pages
    One county's notable sightings from the last 7 or 30 days, with its rarest birds and hotspots.
    California bird species

    Species pages

    Search
    Find a bird, county, or place from the top of any page.

    Labels

    Rarity

    Rarity comes from eBird Status and Trends: how likely birders are to find the species in its best month, averaged across California. The colored dot next to a bird always shows its rarity.

    Rare (no seasonal data)
    Status and Trends has no California data for the species, usually because it's a vagrant that rarely reaches the state. Often the rarest birds of all.
    Rare
    Found on fewer than 0.1% of checklists. A few species were adjusted to match eBird's own labels.
    Uncommon
    Found on 0.1% to 5% of checklists.
    Common
    Found on 5% or more of checklists.
    Escapee or non-native
    Not on the official California list, like escaped pets and released birds.

    California Residency Timeline

    Dark blue months are when the bird is usually in California; gray months are when it usually isn't. "Now" marks today's date, wherever you are. This timeline shows a migrant bird that visits California every season except summer.

      Migration

      Year-round
      In California in every season.
      Winter visitor
      Mostly here in the colder months.
      Summer visitor
      Mostly here in the warmer months, often to breed.
      Migrant
      Passes through in spring or fall.
      Rare visitor / vagrant
      A wild bird that rarely reaches California, too rare for Status and Trends to show a season.

      CBRC review species

      The California Bird Records Committee (CBRC) reviews every sighting of these species to confirm it. Certain foreign birds that appeared in the data needed a manual review to confirm their existence, designated by a CBRC review tag.

      Photos

      Bird photos come primarily from iNaturalist and the Macaulay Library. Photographers share them under Creative Commons licenses, and each photo shows its photographer and license where it appears.
